Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started on Lingauro.

Is Lingauro really free to use?
Yes, Lingauro is completely free. You can drop into open rooms, play icebreaker games, watch videos with partners, and start 1-on-1 exchanges with no payment required.
Do I have to turn on my video camera?
Not at all! You are free to participate voice-only or even start by listening in with your microphone muted until you feel confident enough to say hello.
I'm a complete beginner. Will people be patient with me?
Absolutely. Every room displays a level badge (e.g. Beginner A1-A2), and many rooms are specifically hosted by patient native speakers who love guiding new learners through basic phrases.
Do I need to download an app from Google Play or App Store?
No downloads are necessary. Lingauro is a Progressive Web App (PWA) that runs instantly in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, or Edge. You can easily pin it to your phone's home screen for one-tap access.
How do games and YouTube watch parties work?
Inside any room, any participant or host can open the activities drawer to start Taboo cards, icebreaker questions, or synchronized YouTube video streams. Everyone in the room sees and hears the same content simultaneously!
